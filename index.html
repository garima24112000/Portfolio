Built a backend-agnostic retrieval and RAG benchmarking framework comparing FAISS, Chroma, and Spark across 10K, 30K, and 50K embedded chunks from single-domain and multi-domain datasets on CPU-constrained Google Colab hardware.

Decoding CO2: Statistical Dive into Global Emissions

Analyzed a country-level panel of global CO₂, economic, energy, and population data from 1982 onward to study how environmental and demographic factors relate to GDP and how those relationships changed over time.

Built a reproducible R and RMarkdown pipeline for cleaning, regression-based imputation, feature transformation, and visualization. Applied stepwise multiple regression with diagnostics, K-means clustering with PCA, and ARIMAX forecasting with structural-break analysis to examine country groupings and changing CO₂–GDP dynamics.

The country-level regression explained 69.2% of log-GDP variation, while the three-cluster solution achieved a 0.528 silhouette score. The aggregated model reached R² = 0.9956, and ARIMAX produced 0.24% test MAPE, identifying structural breaks in 1991, 2000, and 2009.

Tech Stack: R | RMarkdown | ggplot2 | dplyr | caret | forecast | strucchange | factoextra | K-means | PCA | ARIMAX

Image Captioning Bot

A multimodal computer vision and NLP system that converts input images into natural-language descriptions. The model was developed and tested using images and reference captions from the Flickr8k dataset.

A pretrained ResNet50 encoder converts each image into a 2,048-dimensional feature representation, while an LSTM decoder combines the visual features with word embeddings to generate captions sequentially.

The pipeline cleans and tokenizes captions, builds a filtered vocabulary, creates padded training sequences, caches extracted image features, and generates captions for unseen images using greedy decoding. Generated captions can also be spoken through a text-to-speech output.

Tech Stack: Python | TensorFlow | Keras | ResNet50 | LSTM | GloVe Embeddings | NumPy | NLTK | Flickr8k | pyttsx3
